## Full description (max 4000)
Learn to speak English with confidence - from the very basics to advanced. Daily English is a complete, offline course that helps you build vocabulary, master grammar, and actually SPEAK, with a unique feature most free apps don't have: you say words and sentences out loud and the app checks your pronunciation.

Perfect for beginners and anyone who wants to improve their spoken English for daily life, work, interviews, and travel.

SPEAK AND GET CHECKED
- Tap the mic, say a word or sentence, and get an instant pronunciation score.
- Hear every word and sentence in a natural American or British voice.
- Slow playback to catch every sound.

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE (beginner to advanced)
- Vocabulary: useful and difficult words with meaning, a simple "how to say it" guide, and examples.
- Grammar Rules: clear lessons on tenses, articles, prepositions, modals, conditionals and more - with spoken examples.
- Everyday Speaking: real sentences English speakers use, grouped by situation.
- Conversations: two-person dialogues (job interview, coffee shop, doctor, phone calls) you can listen to and practice line by line.
- Word Usage: which word to use when - its/it's, say/tell, do/make, much/many and more.
- Phrases & Idioms: common expressions with meaning and examples.

TEST YOURSELF
- Quiz mode with grammar, vocabulary and usage questions, instant answers and explanations.

BUILD A DAILY HABIT
- Word of the Day, daily goal, and a streak counter.
- Daily reminder so you never miss practice.
- Save your favorite words and phrases for quick review.

WHY DAILY ENGLISH
- 100% offline - learn anywhere, no internet needed.
- Simple, easy English made for learners.
- Free to use.

Download Daily English today and start speaking with confidence!
